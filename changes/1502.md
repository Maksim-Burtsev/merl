### Fixed

- `d` on a member through a Scala alias of an outer class (`class Crate { outer =>`, then `outer.pry()` in a nested class) or through `Crate.this` lands on the outer class's member instead of opening a picker, and `d` through a Go struct declared on one line (`type Plain struct{ Base }`) reaches the embedded type's fields and methods (#1502)
- `d` in PHP on `$self->tick()` after `$self = $this;` (also inside a closure that captures it with `use ($self)`) lands on the class's own `tick`, and `$this->tick()` inside `new class { … }` lands on the anonymous class's member, or on the class it extends, instead of opening a picker (#1502)
- `d` in Ruby on `me.tick` after `me = self` in an instance method, also inside a block, lands on the class's own `tick` instead of opening a picker (#1502)
