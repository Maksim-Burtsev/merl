### Fixed

- `d` in Julia no longer lands on the parameter, an earlier local or the module-level namesake when the name is bound again by an enclosing `let`, a `let …;` earlier on the cursor's line, or an assignment ended by `;` earlier on the line (#1396)
- `d` in Julia on a name in the body of a comprehension that spans several lines, `[c.volume` / `for c in pots]`, lands on the `for` line below that binds it, not on the parameter of the same name (#1396)
