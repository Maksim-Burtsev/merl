### Fixed

- `d` in Dart lands on the one declaration instead of a picker of every namesake for: a field of the enclosing class read by its bare name (`$value` or `${value}` in a string, or `value` outside one); a field a parent class declares and makes with a constructor call (`_completer.complete(..)` under `final _completer = Completer<..>();`); the variable of a `for (final entry in fields)` loop over a list whose element type is written (`List<T>`, `Set<T>`, `Iterable<T>`, or a literal `<T>[]`); a member of a list literal (`['gzip', 'deflate'].contains(..)`); and a member an SDK class inherits from a class of its own library (`file.existsSync()` reaching `FileSystemEntity`) (#1299)
