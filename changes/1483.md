### Fixed

- `d` on the member after a property lands on that member instead of a picker of every namesake when the property's type is written: a Dart getter with a setter beside it (`b.size.next()`, bare `size.next()` inside the class), a one-line Kotlin `val size: Cog get() = inner`, and a JavaScript getter typed by JSDoc `@type {Cog}` or `@returns {Cog}`; `d` on `field` inside a Kotlin property's `get()` or `set()` lands on that property (#1483)
- `d` in Dart on a member of a local bound to a getter or field read (`final s = w.slack; s.click()`) lands on the member of the getter's declared type instead of offering every namesake; an untyped getter keeps the picker (#1483)
