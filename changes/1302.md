### Fixed

- `d` in Rust follows a call of a local closure (`let m = |path: &str| gitignore.matched(path, false);` then `m("a").is_ignore()`) to the type its body returns, read from a written `-> T` or from a body that is one expression, so the method after the call lands on its one declaration instead of a picker; a closure whose body has statements, or whose result starts at one of its own parameters, still opens the picker (#1302)
- `d` in Rust on a variant brought by a glob over a dependency's enum (`use regex_syntax::hir::HirKind::*;` then `Capture(..)` in a `match`) opens that variant in the dependency, where it opened a picker of namesakes or jumped to a struct of the same name; a name the file also imports, or a glob over a module rather than an enum, keeps the answer it had (#1302)
- `d` in Rust on a variant reached through a crate of the workspace that hands another crate on (`use grep::printer::SummaryKind;`, where `grep` has `pub extern crate grep_printer as printer;`, then `SummaryKind::PathWithMatch`) opens that variant, where it said "no definition" (#1302)
- `d` in Rust on a path whose type a `use` inside a `mod tests` brings (`use grep_regex::RegexMatcher;` then `RegexMatcher::new(..)`) follows that `use` past a multi-line string the module holds at column 0, so it lands on the one `new` instead of a picker of every `new` (#1302)
