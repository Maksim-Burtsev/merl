### Fixed

- `d` in PHP no longer jumps to the first type of a variable bound again before the use (a second statement on the line, `$a = $x = new T()`, `??=` and the other compound assignments, `instanceof` narrowing to another class, a closure on the line that does not capture it, `use (&$x)`, `unset`, `extract()`, a variable variable, an out-parameter of `preg_match` or `parse_str`, a keyed destructuring, or a `foreach` header or destructuring wrapped over several lines): it offers every namesake instead (#1382)
