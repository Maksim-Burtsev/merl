### Fixed

- `d` in Dart lands on the superclass constructor's parameter from a `super.` parameter, and on the class from a generic type followed by a call on the same line, not on a picker with its constructor (#1338)
- `d` in Dart on a named constructor of a class from outside the project whose first parameter has no type (`ArgumentError.value(x, 'name')`) lands on that constructor in the SDK, not on a project member of the same name or a picker of them; on a method of a field or local set to a typed list literal (`final files = <MapEntry<String, MultipartFile>>[];`, then `files.add(...)`) it lands on `List`'s method, not on a picker of project namesakes (#1338)
- `d` in Dart on a member of the loop variable of a `for` or `await for` over a call of the class's own method (`await for (final chunk in finalize())`, `finalize` returning a `Stream<Uint8List>`) lands on the element type's member, not on a picker of project namesakes (#1338)
- `d` in Dart on a name through an import prefix whose library only hands it on (`web_adapter.compute`, with `dio_web_adapter.dart` a list of `export` lines) lands on the declaration the exports reach, not on a picker of every namesake; on a member of a local set to an entry's `.value` or `.key` (`final file = entry.value;` with `MapEntry<String, MultipartFile> entry`) it lands on that type's member (#1338)
