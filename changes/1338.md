### Fixed

- `d` in Dart lands on the superclass constructor's parameter from a `super.` parameter, and on the class from a generic type followed by a call on the same line, not on a picker with its constructor (#1338)
- `d` in Dart on a named constructor of a class from outside the project whose first parameter has no type (`ArgumentError.value(x, 'name')`) lands on that constructor in the SDK, not on a project member of the same name or a picker of them; on a method of a field or local set to a typed list literal (`final files = <MapEntry<String, MultipartFile>>[];`, then `files.add(...)`) it lands on `List`'s method, not on a picker of project namesakes (#1338)
