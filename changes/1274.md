### Fixed

- `d` on a member whose receiver merl cannot type, in a Vue or Svelte component or a JS/TS file, no longer jumps to a namesake field of another class when a key of an inline object type (`Array<{name: string}>`, a nested `pageData: {…}` of an interface) or a store's `$value` could be the one: those keys are counted and offered in the picker (#1274)
- `d` on a member of a Svelte store's value (`$settings.sortBy`) jumps to the member of the store's type when the store is a `writable<T>` or `readable<T>` of `svelte/store` or a `persisted<T>` of `svelte-persisted-store`, declared once, instead of offering every namesake (#1274)
