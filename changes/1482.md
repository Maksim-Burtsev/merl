### Fixed

- `d` on a property written through a typed receiver (`b.size = new Cog()`, `this.size = …`) lands on its `set size` in TypeScript and JavaScript instead of on `get size`, and in Python on its `@size.setter`; a read (`b.size`, `b.size.next()`, `if b.size:`, `{ size: b.size }`) of a Python `@property` with a setter, or of a TypeScript `get`/`set` pair written over several lines or decorated, lands on the getter instead of a picker of the two; `+=`, `del`, `++` and destructuring keep the picker (#1482)
