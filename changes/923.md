### Added

- `*` finds the word under the cursor in the file as a whole word, in its case, and goes to the next one; `n` / `N` walk the rest, and on a selection in one line it finds the selection as `/` does. In files whose names hold `-`, `.`, `'` or `?` (CSS, HTML, YAML, Make, Terraform, R, Haskell, Nix, the Lisps) and on accented names it can still stop inside a longer name, until #1321 (#923)
