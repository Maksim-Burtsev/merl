### Fixed

- `d` in PHP on a method at the end of a chain that starts with a static call, such as `Album::query()->find(..)` where the model declares `query(): AlbumBuilder`, lands on its one declaration; a link whose method documents `@return $this` or `@return static` (Laravel's query builder) keeps the chain going, and a call with quoted arguments (`->where('a', 'b')`) no longer breaks it. A chain whose first link declares no return type, as `Song::query()` on a model with its own builder, keeps the picker (#1279)
