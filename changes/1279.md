### Fixed

- `d` in PHP on a method at the end of a chain that starts with a static call, such as `Album::query()->find(..)` where the model declares `query(): AlbumBuilder`, lands on its one declaration; a link whose method documents `@return $this` or `@return static` (Laravel's query builder) keeps the chain going, and a call with quoted arguments (`->where('a', 'b')`) no longer breaks it. A chain whose first link declares no return type, as `Song::query()` on a model with its own builder, keeps the picker (#1279)
- `d` in PHP on a static call on a class from `vendor/`, such as Laravel's `Arr::get(..)` or `Collection::wrap(..)`, lands on what that one class, its traits and its parents declare, even when its file sits outside the folder its namespace names (`Illuminate\Support\Arr` lives in `Collections/Arr.php`), where it offered, or jumped to, a namesake method of another class in that namespace such as `Env::get`; on a class name, as in its `use` line, it lands on the class. A member that class, its traits and its parents do not declare (one only `__callStatic` answers), or a class the project also declares with that member, keeps the search over the namespace it had (#1279)
