### Fixed

- `d` on a member of a value checked by `instanceof` (TypeScript, JavaScript) or `isinstance` (Python) lands on the checked type's member inside the check and after an early return on its negation, no longer on the declared base's; a check it cannot read opens the picker (#1493)
- `d` also narrows on a condition wrapped over several lines, after `if (v instanceof T) {…} else { return; }` / `if isinstance(v, T): … else: return`, and on a checked `this.x` / `self.x` attribute; an `else` that does more than exit, or an attribute assigned after the check, opens the picker (#1493)
