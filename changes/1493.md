### Fixed

- `d` on a member of a value checked by `instanceof` (TypeScript, JavaScript) or `isinstance` (Python) lands on the checked type's member inside the check and after an early return on its negation, no longer on the declared base's, when the checked class derives from the declared type (or the declared type is unknown); a check it cannot read, a class outside the project or an unrelated one, and a value assigned again after the check keep the declared type's answer (#1493)
- `d` also narrows on a condition wrapped over several lines, after `if (v instanceof T) {…} else { return; }` / `if isinstance(v, T): … else: return`, and on a checked `this.x` / `self.x` attribute (#1493)
