### Fixed

- `d` in Lua no longer lands on an outer binding, or its table's method, when the name is bound again earlier on the cursor's own line: by a closure's or a loop's parameter, a `local` after `;` or before `until`, or a reassignment (#1393)
- `d` in Lua no longer lands on an outer binding, or its table's method, when the name is bound again on an earlier line: by a `local` after `;`, a reassignment on a line of its own, or a parameter of a list split over two lines (#1393)
