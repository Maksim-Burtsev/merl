### Fixed

- `d` in Julia on a type after `::` (`i::Integer`, `bc::Base.Broadcast.Broadcasted`) looks the type up instead of a member of the value, so a type from Base or a package is found; a type parameter of a `where` or a `struct` still is not (#1375)
- `d` in Julia on the variable of a `catch err` block lands on that `catch` (#1375)
- `d` in Julia on a parametric alias (`AbstractVector`, `AbstractMatrix`) lands on its `const AbstractVector{T} = AbstractArray{T,1}` (#1375)
- `d` in Julia on `Base` (`Base.show(io, x) = ...`) lands on `baremodule Base` instead of a picker that also offered the `const Base` of Base's own `Iterators` and `Order` submodules (#1375)
- `d` in Julia on a field of a parameter typed by a struct (`gd.cols` in `f(gd::GroupedDataFrame)`) lands on that struct's field instead of a picker of every `cols` field; an abstract or `where`-bound type, a reassigned parameter, or a `getproperty` method of the type or its supertypes that may answer the name keeps the picker (#1375)
