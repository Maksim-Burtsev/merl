### Added

- `f` folds Make (`Makefile`, `GNUmakefile`, `.mk`) and CMake (`CMakeLists.txt`, `.cmake`), where it said `no fold rules`: a rule with its recipe, an `ifeq` / `ifdef` … `endif` and a `define` … `endef` in Make; `if`, `foreach`, `while`, `function`, `macro` and `block` in CMake, each from its first line; a line inside a rule, a `define`, a CMake `function` or `macro` folds it.
