### Fixed

- `d` on the label of a named argument in Ruby without parentheses (`pot.brew cup: 4`), in Julia (`brew(; cup = 4)`) and on an Elixir keyword (`brew(cup: 4)`) no longer jumps to a local or a function named like the label: Ruby and Julia land on the parameter, Elixir says it is a key (#1446)
- `d` on a Clojure keyword (`(brew :cup m)`, `(:cup m)`, `::cup`, `{:cup 1}`) and on a key of a Julia named tuple (`(cup = 1, b = 2)`, `(; cup = 1)`) no longer jumps to a local, a parameter or a function named like it (#1446)
- `d` on the label of a named argument in Dart (`brew(cup: 4)`, `pot.brew(cup: 4)`, `Cup.sized(cup: 4)`) lands on the callee's `{…}` parameter or its `this.cup`, instead of a picker of a local, a field and a top-level constant named like it (#1446)
- `d` on the label of a named argument in Ruby lands on the parameter of a method declared without parentheses (`def brew cups, cup: 0`), and on a label after a positional argument of a call without parentheses (`brew 1, cup: 4`, `pot.brew 1, cup: 4, lid: 2`), where it said "argument label" and stayed (#1446)
- `d` on the label of a named argument in Ruby on a continuation line of a call without parentheses (`brew 1,` then `cup: 4` on the next line) lands on the parameter instead of a local named like the label, and a label after a string argument (`brew "a b", cup: 4`) finds its callee, where it said "argument label" and stayed (#1446)
