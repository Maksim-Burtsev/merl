### Fixed

- A selection in a picker's query (`o`, `s`, `T`, …) is drawn like a selection in the code, in the theme's selection colour, as LazyVim draws it: the cursor on its first cell no longer vanishes into it, as it did in github-light, tokyonight-moon, gruvbox and most bundled themes. The `/`, `:` and `new:` prompts keep their reversed band (#1312)
