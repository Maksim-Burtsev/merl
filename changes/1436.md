### Fixed

- `d` in C# on a call that a subclass's own overloads cannot take, by the number of arguments or by an `int` or string literal, lands on the inherited method the call reaches, not on the subclass's overload (#1436)
- `d` in Swift on a call that a subclass's own overloads cannot take, by argument labels or by an `Int`, `String` or `Bool` literal, lands on the inherited method the call reaches, not on the subclass's overload (#1436)
