### Added

- `f` folds Erlang (`.erl`, `.hrl`), where it said `no fold rules for .erl`: a function clause
  from its head to its `;` or `.`, an anonymous or named `fun` … `end`, a `case` or `maybe` …
  `end`, a map `#{` … `}` and a multi-line `-export` / `-export_type`; anywhere else inside a
  function it folds that clause, as Neovim with nvim-treesitter does (#1356)
