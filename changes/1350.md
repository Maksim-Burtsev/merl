### Added

- `f` folds Julia (`.jl`), where it said `no fold rules for .jl`: a `function`, `macro`,
  `module`, `struct`, `if`, `for`, `while`, `try`, `let`, `quote`, `begin` or `do` block to its
  `end`, as Neovim folds it; `end` and `begin` inside an index (`xs[end]`) and the `for` / `if`
  of a comprehension fold nothing. Anywhere else inside a function, f folds the function (#1350)
