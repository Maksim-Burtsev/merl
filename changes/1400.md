### Fixed

- `d` in Objective-C on a name bound again by a block parameter or by a `for (id x in xs)` with no braces lands on that binding, no longer on the outer namesake (#1400)
- `d` in C on a member of a name declared in parentheses (`struct beta (*a) = 0;`) reads that declaration's type, no longer the outer namesake's (#1400)
- `d` in C on a member of a name whose type is a `typedef` inside the function (`typedef struct beta B; B *a = 0; a->get`) lands on that type's member, no longer in a picker of every namesake (#1400)
