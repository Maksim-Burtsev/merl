### Fixed

- `d` in Scala on a name a renaming import binds (`import shop.cups.{Mug => Cup}`, `{Mug as Cup}`) lands on the original class, and a method called on it (`new Cup().pour()`, `val a = Cup(); a.pour()`) on the original's method, instead of on another package's namesake `class Cup`; in Kotlin a method called on a renamed import's constructor (`Cup().pour()`) lands on the original's method instead of a picker; in Elixir `d` on the alias of `alias Shop.Cups, as: C` lands on `defmodule Shop.Cups` instead of a project `defmodule C` (#1466)
- `d` in C# follows a renaming `using Cup = Shop.Cups.Mug;`: `new Cup()`, `Cup` and a receiver built by `new Cup()` land on `Mug`, and a `class Cup` of an enclosing namespace, which hides the alias, no longer sends `new Cup().Pour()` to `Mug.Pour` (#1466)
- `d` in Kotlin on a method called through a typealias (`typealias Kettle = shop.cups.Mug`, then `Kettle().pour()` or `val k = Kettle(); k.pour()`) lands on the aliased class's method instead of a picker (#1466)
- `d` in Go on a method or field of a composite literal (`Teapot{}.Pour()`, `c.Mug{}.Pour()`, and through a type alias, `type Cup = c.Mug` then `Cup{}.Pour()`) lands on that type's member instead of a picker of every namesake (#1466)
- `d` in Swift through a `typealias Cup = Mug` when a target the file does not import declares its own `Cup`: `d` on `Cup` lands on the typealias line, and a method called on a value it builds (`let a = Cup(); a.pour()`, `Cup().pour()`) on `Mug`'s method, instead of pickers (#1466)
- `d` in Kotlin on a method called through an imported typealias (`import shop.Kettle`, or `import shop.Kettle as K`, then `Kettle().pour()`) lands on the aliased class's method instead of a picker (#1466)
- `d` in Scala on a method called on a `val` or `var` built with `new` (`val a = new Cup(); a.pour()`, renamed import or not) lands on that class's method instead of a picker (#1466)
- `d` in Scala through a package renamed by an import (`import shop.{cups => c}`, then `new c.Mug()` or `new c.Mug().pour()`) lands on that package's class and its method, and a method called on a `val` built with type arguments (`val a = new Box[Int](); a.open()`) lands on the class's method, instead of pickers (#1466)
- `d` in Kotlin on a method called through a typealias of a typealias (`typealias Dram = Quaich`, `typealias Quaich = shop.cups.Tankard`, then `Dram().swig()`) lands on the class's method instead of a picker (#1466)
- `d` in TypeScript on a name imported from a barrel that imports it and hands it on in an `export { … }` list (`import presentCollection from "./collection"; export { presentCollection };`), renamed on import or not, lands on its declaration instead of "no definition" (#1466)
- `d` in Elixir on the alias of `alias Ecto.Changeset, as: C`, a module outside the project, no longer jumps to the project's own `defmodule C` (#1466)
- `d` in C# through a `using Sb = System.Text.StringBuilder;` alias of a type outside the project no longer jumps to a project class named `Sb` or its members (`Sb.Create()`, `Sb.Inner.Run()`), and a method called on a value of the alias (`Sb s; s.Append()`) says it is outside the project instead of a picker of namesakes (#1466)
