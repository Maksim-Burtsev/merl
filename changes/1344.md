### Fixed

- `d` in Racket and Scheme on a name a `let-values` binds, or a pattern variable of a `syntax-rules` rule, stays on it or lands on it from its uses instead of jumping to a namesake elsewhere; in Common Lisp `d` stays on a `defgeneric` parameter and lands on a `(:method …)` parameter from that method's body; a Scheme name with `%` in it (`%hash-table-delete!`, Racket's `option-snip%`) is read whole (#1344)
- `d` in an R7RS library on a name its `(import (rename …))` gives, such as `%hash-table-delete!` in chibi-scheme's SRFI 125, lands on the rename in the `.sld` that includes the file instead of saying no definition (#1344)
