### Fixed

- `d` in Scala no longer jumps to the outer type's member on a name bound again by a `for` generator or enumerator, a pattern `val`, `given`, `implicit val` or a colon lambda, or after a closed `case` arm on the same line: it opens the picker (#1389)
- `d` on `this.member` inside a one-line anonymous class (`new Kettle { … this.pour() }`, `object : Kettle() { … }`) in Java, Kotlin and Scala no longer jumps to the outer class's member of that name: it opens the picker (#1389)
