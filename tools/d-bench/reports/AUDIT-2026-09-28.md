## 3. Considered and dropped

- **Leads:**
  - `tree::from_files` O(n²): measured on a 4,000-file branch, the review opens in 1.0 s and a live refresh shows in 1.1 s. Not worth a ticket.
  - Submodule as the first file of a review: fixed; it is now skipped.
  - Row-low cursor on a wider terminal: did not reproduce with a resize (the `t` case is gap 6).
  - Ctrl+N through a symlink is folded into gap 12.
  - The FIFO and site-packages leads are gaps 13 and 19.
- **Contradict a decision or already exist:**
  - `c` skipping viewed files: decided against in #172 and #240.
  - Scrolloff and `@` file symbols: #1, deferred.
  - Copy a `path:line` reference: #160.
  - Review-scoped `o`/`s`/`u`: #171, #246.
  - A path filter for `s`: a toggle.
  - Hide-whitespace toggle: a toggle.
  - Side-by-side diff: merl is a lens over the file, and there are no splits.
  - Review progress bar: rejected earlier.
- **Out of scope:** `d` on a file path in Markdown or strings (`no rules for .md`) belongs to the `d` audit; it pairs with gap 4.
- **Settled trade-offs:**
  - No-index scaling on a synthetic 100k-file repo: `o` answers in 0.14 s, `s` in 3.9 s, live `D` in 7.1 s per query.
  - Two merls quitting at once can lose key counts; documented at `stats.rs:190-193`.
  - inotify limits: watches are retried on each walk; not testable on macOS.
- **Checked and fine:**
  - A 150 MB log opens in 0.3 s (637 MB RSS).
  - Resizing down to 1×1 with overlays open does not crash.
  - Bracketed multi-line paste arrives in one piece.
  - A renamed-and-edited file, merge-conflict markers, `-b HEAD` and `-b HEAD~1` all work in review.
  - A subdirectory opens as its own root.
